# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** *11/04/2025*

## Introduction

This Chrome extension, **tabDirect**, allows users to open a
user-specified web page whenever a new browser tab is created. It is
developed by *qrayg*.

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting it. This
document explains what data the extension handles and how it is used.

## Data Collection and Usage

-   The extension **does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
    information**.\
-   The only data stored is the **URL you choose** in the extension's
    Options page.\
-   This URL is saved **locally in Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync`**,
    which allows it to sync across your own Chrome browsers when you're
    signed in.\
-   The extension **does not send this data to any external servers** or
    analytics systems.

## Permissions

This extension requests the following permission: - **`storage`** ---
Used solely to save and retrieve your chosen custom new tab URL.

It does **not** request access to browsing history, cookies, or any
other private data.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does **not** use or integrate with any third-party
services, trackers, or analytics providers.

## Security

All data stored by the extension remains within your own Chrome profile.
The developer has **no access** to it.

## Changes

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be published in the
extension's listing and version release notes.
